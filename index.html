_about

Curious, hands-on, and focused on usable design.

Hello! My name is Poyi (PY). My background is in History, as well as Management. For over two decades, I have worked with Not-for-Profits, NGOs, and other organisations. Currently, I reside in Portugal with my husband and regularly perform traditional Portuguese folk music with groups in the community.

My work in the digital world sits between experimentation and practical design: web pages, little tools, playful interfaces, and projects that are built to be useful, readable, and easy to explore. This place is a small archive of what I am learning, making, and revisiting — a digital cabinet of experiments, ideas, and public code.
